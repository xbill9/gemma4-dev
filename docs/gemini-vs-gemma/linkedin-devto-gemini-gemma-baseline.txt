Gemini 4 or Gemma 4? It Depends.

Seven models through one Gemini API endpoint, started together with identical settings: Gemma 4 26B and 31B, Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite and 3.8 Flash, and Gemini 4 Hydrogen, Carbon and Argon, scored on GSM8K and BFCL. Hydrogen has the lowest Gemini 4 price per token and cost the most per run, because its thinking cannot be turned off.

What is in it:

- 🟢 Gemini 4 Argon scored highest: 0.9841 on GSM8K and 0.9675 on BFCL simple, for $3.46.
- 🟢 Gemma 4 31B tied Argon on BFCL at 0.9675 and scored 0.9735 on GSM8K, on the free tier.
- 🟢 Hydrogen, Carbon and Argon were ahead of Gemma 4 31B on GSM8K; on BFCL all three were level with it.
- 🟢 Gemma 4 26B was level with the 31B on both tests.
