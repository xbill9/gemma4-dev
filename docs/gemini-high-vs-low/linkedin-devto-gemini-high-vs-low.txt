High or Low Thinking? Gemini 4 and Gemma 4 on Hard Math, Cost First

Seven models on the 15 problems of AIME 2025 I at their lowest and highest thinking settings, under a hard budget. The top setting cost 2.6 to 4.8 times as much on every paid model; Hydrogen, Carbon and Argon solved all 15 at either setting, and no score change was bigger than the run-to-run noise.

What is in it:

- 🟢 Hydrogen, Carbon and Argon solved all 15 problems at both their lowest and highest settings.
- ⚠️ The highest setting cost 2.61 to 4.75 times the lowest on every paid model, $5.95 against $1.53 for all seven.
- ⚠️ No model's score change was bigger than the run-to-run noise; the largest gain was two problems.
- 🟢 Argon had the lowest cost per right answer, $0.0162 at its lowest setting.
