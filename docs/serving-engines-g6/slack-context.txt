Six serving engines for Gemma 4 E2B on one EC2 G6 (g6.4xlarge, one NVIDIA L4): llama.cpp, vLLM, SGLang, mistral.rs, a plain PyTorch engine and LiteRT-LM, same 4-bit weights, same prompts, a 1-to-64-request sweep and GSM8K/BFCL on each.
Answers are level on every engine. vLLM with Google's drafter is the fastest at every load, 275 tokens per second for one request and 4,516 at 64, about $0.08 per million output tokens on demand.
