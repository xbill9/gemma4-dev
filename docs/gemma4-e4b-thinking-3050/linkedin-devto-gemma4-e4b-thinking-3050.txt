Thinking On or Off? Gemma 4 E4B on an 8 GB RTX 3050

Gemma 4 E4B on a local RTX 3050 with thinking off and on, two QAT 4-bit builds, with and without a drafter, on all 1,319 GSM8K problems and the 30 problems of AIME 2025. E4B scored 0.927 to 0.944 on GSM8K; thinking took 2.24 to 2.47 times as long for at most 16 more problems; the drafter cut the time by 1.38 to 2.39 times and changed no score.

What is in it:

- 🟢 E4B scored 0.9272 to 0.9439 on GSM8K and 9 to 13 of 30 on AIME 2025.
- ⚠️ Thinking took 2.24 to 2.47 times as long on GSM8K for 2 to 16 more problems, one of four gains beyond chance.
- ❌ Thinking gave no AIME gain: −4 to +2 problems.
- 🟢 The drafter made every GSM8K run 1.99 to 2.39 times faster and changed no score beyond chance.
