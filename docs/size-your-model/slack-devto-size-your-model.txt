Gemma 4 E2B and E4B, Google's 4-bit QAT small models, served with llama.cpp on an NVIDIA L4 in an EC2 G6, against the same models on an 8 GB RTX 3050 and a 4 GB GTX 1650 Ti.
For one user, the 24 GB L4 runs 1.2-1.3x the 8 GB desktop card with the same answers, because a 4-bit small model uses 13-29% of the L4's memory. The L4 earns its keep with many users at once. Size the model to where you run it.
