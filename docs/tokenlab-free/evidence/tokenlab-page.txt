FAQ:
- Points: "Each model uses points per million tokens at its own rate ... Your key has a points budget for its 30-day access period. Points don't reset daily."
- 5xx / cut-off stream: send again after a few seconds; Retry-After may be present; SDKs retry 5xx (max_retries 2) but not a stream that already started. A finished OpenAI-style stream ends with data: [DONE].
- Out of points: HTTP 429; access period ended: HTTP 403. "Request more points" through the AMD AI Developer Program. "Extending your access (not your points) by 15 days can be requested once, in the last 5 days of your 30-day access period".
- Rate limits: "Up to the limit shown above, in requests per minute, per key. Over the limit you get HTTP 429 with a Retry-After header."
- Lost key: "Regenerate key. The old key stops working immediately and your points and access period carry over."
- Notebooks: "Yes. Calls from AMD AI Notebooks go to the same base URL. Keep your key out of notebooks you share."
