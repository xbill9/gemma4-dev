Which Gemini Model Should You Use? Neon, Argon, Hydrogen, Carbon, Flash or Gemma. It Depends.

Gemini 4 Neon joins seven other models on GSM8K, BFCL and 30 AIME 2025 problems through the Gemini API, with cost per right answer, thinking and speed for each. Every Gemini 4 model solved all 30 hard problems at its lowest setting, so price and speed decide among them; Flash-Lite needs high thinking; Neon is the cheaper, faster choice against Carbon at high thinking.

What is in it:

- 🟢 Hydrogen, Carbon, Argon and Neon solved all 30 AIME 2025 problems at their lowest setting.
- 🟢 Argon cost the least of them per problem, $0.018; Hydrogen answered fastest, 11.3 s.
- 🟢 Neon matched Carbon at high thinking for 0.73 times the cost.
- 🟢 Neon has the best function-calling score, 0.9750, level with Argon and Gemma 4 31B.
