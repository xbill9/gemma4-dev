Jev has had eight days of independent tests: arXiv preprints, GitHub evaluations and blog benchmarks.
I traced every figure to its primary source and re-scored the ones with committed per-item outputs.
On accuracy it sits level with mid-price LLMs and 6.5 to 11.5 points behind the frontier in the cleanest comparison.
Out of the box it is the best calibrated of the models measured on familiar English tasks, and one temperature fitted on 50 to a few hundred labels fixes most of the error.
