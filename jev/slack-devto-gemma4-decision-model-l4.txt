Jev launched as a new kind of decision model, and the open question was how close a plain open model read the same way gets.
I read Gemma 4 26B by its label probabilities on one EC2 g6.xlarge L4 and set it beside Jev's published results on the same 3,880 public records.
It lands 2.1 points behind overall and level on yes/no, and one temperature fitted on 50 labels brings its calibration within 0.01 of Jev's.
