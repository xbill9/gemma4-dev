  PREFILL graph : Option B forward, also returns the 15 non-shared layers' K/V.
                  (shared layers 15-34 never write cache — HF modeling_gemma4 L1273;
                   cache.layers[i].keys/.values is the transformers-5.13 API)

                  explicit tensor I/O. Custom StaticKV.update = one-hot masked write
                  buf*(1-oh)+k*oh (pure arithmetic, trace-safe). Position/masks/onehot
                  precomputed on host -> zero int-index ops in the graph. attention_mask
                  passed as a dict {full_attention,sliding_attention} to bypass
                  create_causal_mask. KV-sharing handled by TextModel.forward internally.
