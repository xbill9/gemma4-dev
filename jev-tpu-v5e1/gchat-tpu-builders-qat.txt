Two papers on Google's quantization-aware-trained Gemma 4 weights on one TPU chip, repacked into formats vLLM serves on the JAX path. The repacks are on Hugging Face.

Gemma 4 QAT on One TPU v5e: What Runs and What Doesn't

🟢 The repacks serve every Gemma 4 size from E2B to 26B on one v5e chip, level with bf16 through 12B
🟢 12B fits at 11.31 GiB and serves 675 output tokens per second, with 0.964 on GSM8K and 0.955 on BFCL
🟢 Up to 2.4 points above Google's own 4-bit exports, at the same speed
❌ bf16 above E2B, 31B in any build, and 26B past a 2,176-token context

Google's QAT Gemma 4 26B-A4B on One TPU v6e: 15.6x the KV Cache and 1.9x the Throughput of FP8

🟢 The QAT 26B serves on one v6e chip at 17.43 GiB, against 27.99 GiB for RedHat's FP8 build
🟢 53,888 tokens of KV cache against 3,456, and 1,283 output tokens per second against 668
⚠️ Against bf16 the suite reads 1.1 points lower on the TPU
