Not missing the JEV hype train 🚂 — this stop is a TPU.

Jev-style decisions on one TPU v6e chip: Gemma 4 read by its label probabilities with vLLM, checked against the same read on an NVIDIA L4 and against Jev 1.13.0's published results.

🟢 One v6e chip serves Gemma 4 E2B, E4B and 12B at bf16 and a 26B-A4B fp8 build
🟢 Same checkpoints, same answers on the TPU and the L4: 1,186 and 1,193 of 1,200
🟢 The 26B decides in 27 to 33 ms on the TPU against 61 ms on the L4
❌ No 31B checkpoint loads today: the w4a16 and 4-bit builds fail on the JAX path, and the fp8 build is 30.98 GiB
⚠️ On demand, the TPU costs more per decision than the L4 or Jev; 12B is the size to pick
