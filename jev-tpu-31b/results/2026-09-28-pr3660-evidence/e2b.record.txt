--- Explain in two sentences why the sky is blue.
The sky appears blue because of a phenomenon called Rayleigh scattering, where sunlight is scattered by the Earth's atmosphere. Shorter wavelengths of light, like blue and violet, are scattered more effectively than longer wavelengths, making the sky look blue to our eyes.
