--- Explain in two sentences why the sky is blue.
The sky appears blue because of a phenomenon called Rayleigh scattering, where the Earth's atmosphere scatters shorter wavelengths of light, such as blue and violet, more effectively than longer wavelengths. This scattered blue light reaches our eyes from all directions, making the sky appear blue.
