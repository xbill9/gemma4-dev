Can a 2021 laptop with a 4 GB GPU run Gemma 4?

I ran Gemma 4 E2B on a Lenovo Yoga 9 that has been on sale since January 2021, with a 4 GB GTX 1650 Ti, and managed it from Claude Code through a Python MCP server.

In bfloat16 the model is 9.5 GiB of weights. Even int8 would not fit the card. Google's quantization-aware-trained checkpoint does: a 3.35 GB GGUF, of which only 1.31 GiB ever has to be on the GPU, because llama.cpp leaves the largest tensor in host memory.

It loaded in 1618 MiB of 4096 and decodes at 73.75 tok/s on a GPU with no tensor cores.

Quantizing further was a loss. A q8_0 KV cache cost 11-12% of decode speed, because QAT had already taken the memory pressure away.
